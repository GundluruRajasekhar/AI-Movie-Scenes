# The Price of Love – HTML Cinematic Film (v5)

7-minute Human Relationships film in pure HTML/CSS/JS. Cost: ₹0.

> If relationships depend on love, humanity lives forever.

## Run
Open `index.html`, press Play (use system audio for voice and music).

## What's new
Full cast (Arjun, Lakshmi, Priya, her parents, Google Director, relatives), Mother's Sacrifice scene, rain rejection scene, company success visuals, receipt twist with flashback, hospital climax, and the "only my mother asked if I had eaten" speech.

## v3 features
Voice picker, jump-to-scene menu, shortcuts (Space, F, M, V, C, L, arrows), fullscreen, letterbox bars, film grain, scene image upload with Ken Burns zoom, emotion meter, end credits with Replay, and .srt subtitle download.

## v4: story and sound
New flashback details (graduation with no chain, skipped meals, sewing at night), line-by-line music levels, and silent beats before the key lines, including "But only my mother asked if I had eaten."

## v5: photos and characters
Photos are embedded in index.html, so there are still only four files. Uday is added as Arjun's friend. To swap any picture, use the Scene image menu or replace the base64 data in index.html.

## Make the MP4
Screen-record full screen with system audio, trim in CapCut, export 1080p 16:9.
